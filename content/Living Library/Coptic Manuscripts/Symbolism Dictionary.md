## A
**Aeon:** *Aeons are personifications of divine attributes, **spiritual realms**, or orders of angels that emanate from the unknowable Godhead.*
*There are twelve aeons + (and their respective archons) that the soul will come across when leaving the body after death.*

*The **sixth aeon** is called the Little Midst, for it belongs to the six aeons which have believed. But the archons of those places have a little goodness within them, because the archons of those places have believed.*

*The **twelfth aeon** is the place of the invisible God with the Barbelo and the unbegotten God. And the invisible God is in a place alone in the twelfth aeon. And veils are drawn before him. For there are many other gods in that aeon who in the Treasury of the Light are called archons; they are the great archons who rule over all the aeons. It is they who serve the invisible God and the Barbelo and the unhegotten one.*

*The **thirteenth aeon** is the place of the great invisible God there with the great virgin spirit and the 24 emanations of the invisible God.*

*In the **fourteenth aeon**, there is the second great invisible God. And the great God is there who is called in the fourteenth aeon: the great beneficent God. He is further more a power of these three archons of the light, which are within all the aeons, namely the three gods which are outside the Treasury of the Light. For there is a multitude of powers in that aeon. But they are not so numerous as those which are in the aeons outside of them. This is this place of these three archons (which are the last of all the aeons) which are within all these invisible ones, namely the triple-powered gods which are outside the Treasury of the Light, that is, the archons of the light - for those three archons are inside all the aeons, and they which are outside all the treasuries are superior to all the gods which are in all the aeons. They also have received the mysteries of the Treasury of the Light, because when the first power came forth, these were the first which remained in it, and when they came down the Kingdom of the Light was preached to them. It (the first power) also gave to them these mysteries but they have not seen the mystery of the forgiveness of sins. Because of this they have not yet been taken into the Treasury of the Light, because they have not yet received the mystery of the forgiveness of sins. There is no place of correction in those places (aeons), because those of that place have received the mysteries, nor is it possible for them to punish you in those places.*

**Aeon of Light:** *typically refers to **a divine, eternal spiritual realm**, or a living personification of the Divine Light emanating directly from the highest, unknowable God.*

**Archon:** *Literally translates to **"ruler," "master," or "authority.** In the cosmological texts it was repurposed to describe malevolent, otherworldly beings that serve as the "wardens" of the material universe.
Archons are the secondary, often blind creators who built the physical cosmos (the material world). They view the physical universe not as a sacred creation, but as a trap designed to imprison the divine spark (the human soul). 
They rule over the planets, the zodiac, and the concept of earthly fate (heimarmene). They manipulate humans through fear, grief, and worldly desires to keep them spiritually blind and unaware of their true, divine origin.

**Note:** *On the book two of Jeu are mentioned **72 archons***.

**The three Baptisms:** *The three baptisms consist of the Baptisms of life, fire and the Holy Spirit.* There are various elements like certain clothing, plants and so on used in the ritual but here is the general description of each.

1. **Baptism of Water:** *The parastatai, or **the fifteen helpers** which serve the seven virgins of the light which are over the baptism of life, are called upon rituals to baptise people in the water of life of the seven virgins of the light and forgive their sins, and purify their iniquities and number them among the inheritance of the Kingdom of the Light.*

2. **Baptism of Fire:** *Zorokothora Melchisedek comes in secret and bring the water of the baptism of fire of the Virgin of the Light, the judge. This is used to forgive a person sins, and make them to be purfied from their iniquities. those which they have committed knowingly and those which they have committed unknowingly, those which they have committed from (their) childhood until today. And (do thou erase) their slanders and their curses and their false oaths and their thefts and their lies and their false accusations and their fornications and their adulteries and their desires and their avarice and those things which they have done from their youth until today. Do thou erase everything. And do thou purify them all.*
   
3. **The Baptism of the Holy Spirit:** *Used to forgive the sins of people and erase their iniquities, those which they have committed knowingly and those which they have committed unknowingly, those which they have committed from their childhood until today. And do thou make them to be numbered within the inheritance of the Kingdom of the Light. In this case Jesus called upon his imperishable names of the Treasury of the Light and used the seal of the seven virgins of the light.*

- **Role:** *They are harsh enforcers, purgative entities, and tormentors.
- **Process:** *The texts describe them as the administrators of the underworld (often referred to as Amente). After physical death, they take souls deemed unrepentant or sinful to be punished and purified by fire over varying, specified periods.
