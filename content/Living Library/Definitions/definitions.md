# Mazda

Mazda / Ahura Mazda is the «Lord of Wisdom», source of order and wisdom. The supreme creator deity in Zoroastrianism. Is the uncreated, eternal spirit responsible for maintaining cosmic order and opposing the destructive chaos.

---

# Ox-Soul

The Ox-Soul (Avestan: Geush Urvan) is the personified soul of living creation in the Gathas, symbolized by cattle. It expresses the suffering of life under violence and disorder and calls for restoration through truth and right order.

---

# Vohu Manah

Is the principle of “Good Thought” in the Gathas, representing ethical awareness, clear thinking, and the inner state that aligns human beings with truth (Asha) and right action.

---

# Asha

Is the fundamental principle of truth, right order, and reality in the Gathas. It refers to the way things truly are and ought to be, governing cosmic order, moral integrity, and right action in both the material and spiritual worlds.

---

# Ahura

A title meaning “Lord” or “Wise Being.” Ahura Mazda is the supreme Ahura, but the Gathas also speak of “other Ahuras,” referring to exalted divine beings aligned with Asha (Truth and Right Order) who participate in the cosmic order under Mazda’s authority.

---

# Vishtaspa

Vishtaspa was a historical king and the main royal patron of Zarathushtra. He supported Zarathushtra’s teachings, protected him, and helped spread the worship of Ahura Mazda, playing a key role in the early Zoroastrian community.

---

# Frashaoshtra

Frashaoshtra was a close companion and devoted supporter of Zarathushtra, belonging to the Hvogva family. He assisted in spreading Zarathushtra’s teachings and upheld the principles of truth (Asha) and good thought (Vohu Manah) in the early Zoroastrian community.
