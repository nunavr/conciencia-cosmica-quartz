|1. _Now I will proclaim to those who will hear the things that the understanding man should remember, for hymns unto [Ahura](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/ahura/) and prayers to Good Thought; also the felicity that is with the heavenly lights, which through Right shall be beheld by him who wisely thinks._  <br>  <br>2. _Hear with your ears the best things; look upon them with clear-seeing thought, for decision between the two Beliefs, each man for himself before the Great consummation, bethinking you that it be accomplished to our pleasure._  <br>  <br>3. _Now the two primal Spirits, who reveal themselves in vision as Twins, are the Better and the Bad, in thought and word and action. And between these two the wise ones chose aright, the foolish not so._  <br>  <br>4. _And when these twain Spirits came together in the beginning, they created Life and Not-Life, and that at the last Worst Existence shall be to the followers of the Lie, but the Best Existence to him that follows Right._  <br>  <br>5. _Of these twain Spirits he that followed the Lie chose doing the worst things; the holiest Spirit chose Right, he that clothes him with the massy heavens as a garment. neighboring possession that now the Liar holds._  <br>  <br>4. _I will worship you with praise, O [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/) [Ahura](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/ahura/), joined with Right and Best Thought and Dominion, that they, desired of pious men, may stand as Judges on the path of the obedient unto the [House of Song](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/house-of-song/)._  <br>  <br>5. _Assured by you, O [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/) [Ahura](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/ahura/) and Right, are the pointings of the hand – since you are well-disposed to your prophet – which shall bring us to bliss, together with visible manifest help._  <br>  <br>6. _The prophet Zarathushtra, O [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/), who lifts up his voice in worship, as friend of [Asha](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/asha/) – may the Creator of Wisdom teach me his ordinances through Good Thought, that my tongue may have a pathway._  <br>  <br>7. _For you I will harness the swiftest steeds, stout and strong, by the promptings of your praise, that ye may come hither, O [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/), Right, and Good thought._  <br>  <br>8. _Ever with verses that are recognized as those of pious zeal I will come before you with outstretched hands, O [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/), before you, O thou Right, with worship of the faithful man, before you with all the capacity of Good Thought._  <br>  <br>9. _With those prayers I would come and praise you, O [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/) and thou Right, with actions of Good Thought. If I be master of my own destiny as I will, then will I take thought for the portion of the wise in the same._  <br>  <br>10. _Those actions that I shall achieve and those done aforetime, and those, O Good Thought, that are precious in thy sight, the rays of the sun, the shimmering uprisings of the days, all is for your praise, O thou Right and [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/) [Ahura](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/ahura/)._  <br>  <br>11. _Your praiser, [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/), will I declare myself and be, so long, O Right, as I have strength and power. May the Creator of the world accomplish through Good Thought its fulfillment of all that most perfectly answers to his will._|1. _(Zarathushtra:) Can my soul rely on anyone for help? Who will protect my herd, and who will protect me? Who will answer my call besides Right, you, O [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/) [Ahura](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/ahura/), and the Best Thought?_  <br>  <br>2. _How, O [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/), should one properly seek the blessing of cattle — one who desires them to come together with good pasture?  <br>**([Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/) replies):** Those who live uprightly according to Right among the many who look upon the sun — those whom they judge — I will establish in the dwellings of the wise._  <br>  <br>3. _(Zarathushtra:) Through Right, O [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/), that reward shall come — the reward you promised through Dominion and Good Thought — to the one whom destiny empowers to reclaim the neighboring lands that the Liar now holds._  <br>  <br>4. _I will worship you with praise, O [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/) [Ahura](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/ahura/), together with Right, Best Thought, and Dominion, so that they — desired by the righteous — may stand as judges along the path that leads the faithful to the [House of Song](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/house-of-song/)._  <br>  <br>5. _By you, O [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/) [Ahura](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/ahura/) and Right, the guiding signs are assured — since you are favorable to your prophet — signs that will lead us to blessedness with clear and visible help._  <br>  <br>6. _The prophet Zarathushtra lifts his voice in worship to you, O [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/), as a friend of Right. May the Creator of Wisdom teach me his laws through Good Thought, so that my tongue may find the proper path to speak them._  <br>  <br>7. _For you I will harness the swiftest horses — strong and powerful — inspired by praise of you, so that you may come here: you, O [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/), Right, and Good Thought._  <br>  <br>8. _With hymns known as expressions of sincere devotion, I will come before you with uplifted hands, O [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/) — before you, O Right — offering the worship of the faithful and the full power of Good Thought._  <br>  <br>9. _With these prayers I come to praise you, O [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/) and Right, through the actions of Good Thought. If I truly hold mastery over my destiny, then I will devote my efforts to securing the share of the wise._  <br>  <br>10. _All the deeds I perform — those of the present and those done before — and all things precious to Good Thought: the rays of the sun and the shining rising of the days — all are offered in praise to you, O Right and [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/) [Ahura](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/ahura/)._  <br>  <br>11. _I declare myself your worshipper, O [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/), and I will remain so as long as I have strength and power, O Right. May the Creator of the world bring to completion, through Good Thought, whatever most perfectly fulfills his will._|

## VOHUKHSHATHRA GATHA (Y51)

|VOHUKHSHATHRA GATHA (Y51)|VOHUKHSHATHRA GATHA (Y51)|

|51 (Original)|51 (Modern English)|
|1. _The good, the precious Dominion, as a most surpassing portion, shall Right achieve for him that with zeal accomplishes what is best through his actions, O [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/). This will I now work out for us._  <br>  <br>2. _Before all, O [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/) [Ahura](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/ahura/), assure me the Dominion of your possession, O Right, and what is thine, O Piety. Your (Dominion) of blessing give through Good Thought to him that prays._  <br>  <br>3. _Let your ears attend to those who in their deeds and utterances hold to your words, [Ahura](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/ahura/) and Right, to those of Good Thought, for whom thou, [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/), art the first teacher._  <br>  <br>4. _Where is the recompense for wrong to be found, where pardon for the same? Where shall they attain the Right? Where is the holy Piety, where Best Thought? Thy Dominions, where are they, O [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/)?_  <br>  <br>5. _All this (I) ask, whether the husbandman shall find cattle in accordance with Right, he that is perfect in action, a man of understanding, when he prays to him, who hath promised unto the Wise the true Judge, in that he is Lord of the two destinies…_  <br>  <br>6. _Even he, [Ahura](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/ahura/) [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/), who through his Dominion appoints what is better than good to him that is attached to his will, but what is worse than evil to him that obeys him not, at the last end of life._  <br>  <br>7. _Give me, O thou that didst create the Ox and Waters and Plants, Welfare and Immortality, by the Highest Spirit, O [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/), strength and continuance through Good Thought at the (Judge’s) sentence._  <br>  <br>8. _Of these two things will I speak, O [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/), – for one may speak to the wise, – the ill that is threatened to the Liar, and the happiness of him who clings to the Right. For he, the Prophet, is glad of every one who says this to the wise._  <br>  <br>9. _What recompense thou wilt give to the two parties by thy red Fire, by the molten metal, give us a sign of it in our souls – even the bringing of ruin to the Liar, of blessing to the Righteous._  <br>  <br>10. _Whoso, other than this one, seeks to kill me, [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/), he is a son of the Lie’s creation, ill-willed thus towards all that live. I call the Right to come to me with good destiny._  <br>  <br>11. _What man is friend to [Spitama Zarathushtra](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/spitama-zarathushtra/), O [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/)? Who will let himself be counseled by Right? With whom is holy Piety? Or who as an upright man is intent on the brotherhood of Good Thought?_  <br>  <br>12. _The [Kavi](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/kavi/)’s wanton did not please [Zarathushtra Spitama](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/zarathushtra-spitama/) at the Winter Gate, in that he stayed him from taking refuge with him, and when there came to him also (Zarathushtra’s) two steeds shivering with cold._  <br>  <br>13. _Thus the Self of the Liar destroys for himself the assurance of the right Way; whose soul shall tremble at the Revelation on the Bridge of the Separator, having turned aside with deeds and tongue from the Path of Right._  <br>  <br>14. _The [Karapans](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/karapans/) will not obey the statutes and ordinances concerning husbandry. For the pain they inflict upon the cattle, fulfill upon them through their actions and judgments that judgment which at the last shall bring them to the House of the Lie._  <br>  <br>15. _When Zarathushtra hath promised to the men of his brotherhood, (which) in the [House of Song](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/house-of-song/) [Ahura](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/ahura/) [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/) hath first attained, for all this I have looked through your blessings, Good Thought, and those of Right._  <br>  <br>16. _[Kavi](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/kavi/) [Vishtaspa](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/vishtaspa/) hath the creed which the holy [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/) [Ahura](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/ahura/) with Right hath devised, together with the dominion of the Brotherhood, and the path of Good Thought. So be it accomplished after our desire._  <br>  <br>17. _The fair form of one that is dear hath [Frashaoshtra](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/frashaoshtra/) Hvogva promised unto me: may sovereign [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/) [Ahura](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/ahura/) grant that she attain possession of the Right for her good Self._  <br>  <br>18. _This creed [Jamaspa Hvogva](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/jamaspa-hvogva/) chooses through Right, lordly in substance. This Dominion they (choose) who have part in Good Thought. This grant me, [Ahura](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/ahura/), that they may find in thee, [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/), their protection._  <br>  <br>19. _The man himself, O [Maidyoi-Maungha Spitama](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/maidyoi-maungha-spitama/), hath set this before him after conceiving it in his own self. He that would see life indeed, to him will he make known what in actions by [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/)’s ordinance is better during (this) existence._  <br>  <br>20. _Your blessings shall ye give us, all that are one in will, with whom Right, Good Thought, Piety, and [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/) (are one) according to promise, giving your aid when worshipped with reverence._  <br>  <br>21. _By Piety the man becomes Holy. Such person advances Right through his thinking, his words, his action, his Self. By Good Thought [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/) [Ahura](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/ahura/) will give the Dominion. For this good Destiny I long._  <br>  <br>22. _He, I ween, that [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/) [Ahura](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/ahura/) knoweth among all that have been and are, as one to whom in accordance with Right the best portion falls for his prayer, these will I reverence by their own names and go before them with honor._|1. _The noble and precious Dominion belongs to the one who, with devotion, performs the best deeds in harmony with [Asha](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/asha/), O [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/). This is the goal I seek to establish among us._  <br>  <br>2. _Grant me first, O [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/) [Ahura](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/ahura/), the share of your Dominion. Let [Asha](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/asha/) and [Armaiti](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/armaiti/) also grant their blessings. Through Good Thought, give this gift to the one who sincerely prays._  <br>  <br>3. _Let your ears hear those who follow your teachings through their words and deeds—those guided by Good Thought. For them, O [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/), you are the first and greatest teacher._  <br>  <br>4. _Where is justice for wrongdoing, and where forgiveness? Where is [Asha](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/asha/), where holy [Armaiti](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/armaiti/), where Good Thought? Where are your divine dominions, O [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/)?_  <br>  <br>5. _I ask this: will the faithful farmer, the one who acts rightly and with understanding, receive cattle and prosperity according to [Asha](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/asha/)? He prays to the true Judge, the Lord who governs the two destinies._  <br>  <br>6. _[Ahura](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/ahura/) [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/), through his Dominion, grants better than good to those aligned with his will, and worse than evil to those who reject it—this judgment comes at the end of life._  <br>  <br>7. _O Creator of the Ox, the Waters, and the Plants, grant me Welfare and Immortality. Let our souls understand the fate of the Liar and the blessing of the righteous._  <br>  <br>10. _Whoever seeks to destroy me, O [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/), is a child of the Lie, hostile toward all living beings. I call upon [Asha](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/asha/) to bring me a favorable destiny._  <br>  <br>11. _Who will stand as a friend to [Spitama Zarathushtra](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/spitama-zarathushtra/), O [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/)? _Who will let himself be counseled by Right? With whom is holy Piety?_? Who joins the fellowship guided by Good Thought?_  <br>  <br>12. _The reckless [Kavi](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/kavi/) did not welcome Zarathushtra at the Winter Gate. He refused him shelter, even as the prophet’s horses shivered in the cold._  <br>  <br>13. _The soul of the Liar destroys its own path. Because of the suffering they cause, their own actions will lead them to the House of the Lie._  <br>  <br>15. _Zarathushtra promises his companions the reward that [Ahura](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/ahura/) [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/) first attained in the [House of Song](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/house-of-song/). For this blessing I rely on Good Thought and [Asha](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/asha/)._  <br>  <br>16. _[Kavi](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/kavi/) [Vishtaspa](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/vishtaspa/) has embraced the faith established by [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/) [Ahura](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/ahura/) through [Asha](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/asha/). May the dominion of this brotherhood and the path of Good Thought flourish as we desire._  <br>  <br>17. _[Frashaoshtra](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/frashaoshtra/) Hvogva has promised me the hand of a beloved woman. May [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/) [Ahura](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/ahura/) grant that she attain [Asha](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/asha/) and spiritual well-being._  <br>  <br>18. _[Jamaspa Hvogva](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/jamaspa-hvogva/) has chosen this faith through [Asha](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/asha/), noble in spirit. Those who share in Good Thought choose this Dominion. Grant this to me, O [Ahura](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/ahura/), that they may find protection in you._  <br>  <br>19. _[Maidyoi-Maungha Spitama](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/maidyoi-maungha-spitama/) has embraced this teaching with his own understanding. To those who seek true life, he reveals the better path established by [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/)’s law._  <br>  <br>20. _Bestow your blessings upon us—all who are united in will with [Asha](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/asha/), Good Thought, [Armaiti](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/armaiti/), and [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/)—when we worship with reverence._  <br>  <br>21. _Through [Armaiti](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/armaiti/) a person becomes holy. Such a person advances [Asha](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/asha/) through thought, word, action, and the self. Through Good Thought, [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/) [Ahura](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/ahura/) grants Dominion. For this good destiny I long._  <br>  <br>22. _Those whom [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/) [Ahura](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/ahura/) knows—among all who have lived and who live—as worthy through [Asha](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/asha/) and their prayers, these I will honor by name and approach with reverence._|

## About the Gathas

### What are the Gathas?

The **Gathas** are a collection of sacred hymns composed by **Zarathushtra**, the prophet and founder of the ancient Iranian religion now known as Zoroastrianism, who likely lived around 1300 BCE, roughly 3,500 years ago. They represent the oldest and most important part of the Zoroastrian scriptures.

The hymns are written in an ancient poetic style using the metrical forms of early Indo-Iranian religious poetry. Their language is highly condensed and symbolic, often omitting normal grammatical structures, which makes interpretation challenging.

In total, the Gathas contain about **6,000 words arranged in roughly 1,300 lines**, organized into **238 verses across 17 chapters**. These chapters are called **Hāiti** (or **Ha**) and were later incorporated into the **Yasna**, the central Zoroastrian liturgical ceremony, which contains 72 chapters in total.

The seventeen Gathic chapters are traditionally grouped into **five sections**:

- **Ahunavaiti Gatha** — Yasna 28–34 (100 verses)
- **Ushtavaiti Gatha** — Yasna 43–46 (66 verses)
- **Spentamainyu Gatha** — Yasna 47–50 (41 verses)
- **Vohu [Khshathra](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/khshathra/) Gatha** — Yasna 51 (22 verses)
- **Vahishtoishti Gatha** — Yasna 53 (9 verses)

The language of the Gathas is known as **Gathic** or **Old Avestan**, one of the oldest Indo-European languages. Much of what scholars understand about it comes from its close relationship with **Vedic Sanskrit**, another ancient Indo-Iranian language.

### The Content of the Gathas

The **Gathas** are poetic hymns addressed both to **[Ahura](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/ahura/) [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/)** and to the people listening to Zarathushtra’s message. They do not present a systematic doctrine in one place; instead, their teachings appear throughout the different hymns. In some passages Zarathushtra explains aspects of his theology, while in others he urges his audience to live according to the divine order established by [Ahura](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/ahura/) [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/). Many verses are devotional, praising the divine principles such as **[Asha](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/asha/)** (Truth), **[Vohu Manah](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/vohu-manah/)** (Good Mind), and **[Armaiti](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/armaiti/)** (Piety). Other verses refer to moments from the prophet’s mission and the challenges he faced while spreading his teachings.

### The Theology of the Gathas

Zarathushtra’s religion can be described as a reflective faith that combines a **vision of the world** with a **way of life**. Believers are encouraged to understand this vision and freely choose to live according to it.

In the Gathas, the world is portrayed as a moral arena where two opposing spiritual forces are present: the spirit aligned with **goodness** and the spirit aligned with **falsehood**. At the center of this worldview is **[Asha](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/asha/)**, often translated as _Truth_ or _Righteousness_. [Asha](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/asha/) represents the ideal order of existence as intended by [Ahura](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/ahura/) [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/)—the perfect state toward which the world should move.

Although the world has been affected by falsehood and disorder, humans possess the ability to improve it through **moral choice**. Each person has the capacity of **[Vohu Manah](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/vohu-manah/)** (Good Mind), which allows them to recognize truth and understand how reality differs from its ideal state. This awareness inspires **good thoughts**, which lead to **good words** and ultimately to **good deeds**, guided by **[Armaiti](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/armaiti/)**—the spirit of devotion, right-mindedness, and benevolence.

Through such actions, humans help bring the world closer to its ideal condition. This process contributes to the realization of **[Khshathra](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/khshathra/) Vairya**, the ideal dominion or just social order where harmony and justice prevail.

A person who lives according to this path achieves **[Haurvatat](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/haurvatat/)** (wholeness or well-being) in life and, after death, **[Ameretat](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/ameretat/)** (immortality). The soul then faces judgment symbolized by crossing the **Chinvat Bridge**, where the righteous pass to the **[House of Song](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/house-of-song/)**, a state of blessed consciousness, while the wicked fall into the **House of the Lie**.

Ultimately, the Gathas teach that the world, though imperfect, can be transformed through enlightened human thought and moral action, fulfilling the purpose envisioned by [Ahura](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/ahura/) [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/).

### The Non-Theological Content of the Gathas

In addition to theology, the Gathas contain devotional prayers, personal reflections, and glimpses of Zarathushtra’s life. Many hymns express reverence for [Ahura](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/ahura/) [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/) and ask for guidance, wisdom, and strength to live righteously.

Some verses also reflect the struggles of the prophet’s mission. Zarathushtra describes opposition from powerful leaders, rejection by his own community, and periods of doubt about whether his message would be accepted. In these moments he seeks reassurance from [Ahura](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/ahura/) [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/) and affirms his trust in the power of truth.

Because the hymns were composed at different stages of his life, they reveal both the difficulties and the eventual success of his mission. Later passages express greater confidence and hope. Even in everyday situations—such as the hymn associated with the wedding of his youngest daughter—Zarathushtra continues to teach the principles of the religion of [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/), showing his lifelong dedication to spreading its message.

### Synopsis of the Gathas

Although the same theological vision runs throughout the Gathas, individual hymns emphasize particular themes. Together they present Zarathushtra’s message about the nature of the world, the moral responsibility of humanity, and the path toward a righteous society.

The Gathas begin with a symbolic account of Zarathushtra’s calling. In **Yasna 29**, the “Soul of the Cow”—representing suffering creation—appeals to [Ahura](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/ahura/) [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/) for protection in a violent world. Zarathushtra is chosen to bring divine wisdom to humanity, teaching a way of life based on truth and moral responsibility.

The early hymns of the **Ahunavaiti Gatha (Y28–34)** introduce the central ideas of the religion: the importance of **[Asha](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/asha/)** (truth and righteousness), **[Vohu Manah](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/vohu-manah/)** (Good Mind), and the human capacity to choose between good and evil. Zarathushtra calls on people to listen thoughtfully and freely choose the path that promotes truth and improves the world. These hymns also condemn violent practices and false teachings that harm both society and the natural world.

The **Ushtavaiti Gatha (Y43–46)** reflects Zarathushtra’s personal experience as a prophet. These hymns include prayers, reflections on divine revelation, and questions addressed to [Ahura](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/ahura/) [Mazda](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/mazda-ahura-mazda/) about the nature of creation and the proper way to live. They also reveal the struggles Zarathushtra faced when his message was rejected by some and gradually accepted by others.

The **[Spenta](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/spenta/) Mainyu Gatha (Y47–50)** focuses on the moral and spiritual forces at work in the world. It emphasizes the role of the **Bounteous Spirit ([Spenta](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/spenta/) Mainyu)**, which inspires right thinking and right action. These hymns reaffirm that through wisdom, righteousness, and dedication to truth, humanity can overcome injustice and help bring about a better world.

The **Vohu [Khshathra](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/glossary/khshathra/) Gatha (Y51)** turns to the idea of an ideal social order. It teaches that leaders and communities must be guided by truth, wisdom, and right-mindedness in order to create a just and harmonious society aligned with the divine order.

Finally, the **Vahishtoishti Gatha (Y53)** concludes the collection with teachings connected to the marriage of Zarathushtra’s daughter. Even in this personal setting, the prophet emphasizes the same principles that run throughout the Gathas: thoughtful choice, mutual respect, and a life guided by **good thoughts, good words, and good deeds**.

Taken together, the Gathas present Zarathushtra’s vision of a world where human beings, guided by truth and wisdom, actively participate in transforming existence toward justice, harmony, and spiritual fulfillment.

## FAQ

**Where to read the Gathas in English?**

You can read the original text side by side with a modern adaptation of the Gathas («Hymns») of Zarathushtra, online, in [our page](https://concienciacosmica.page.gd/the-gathas-the-divine-songs-of-zarathushtra/). Alternative options are in the following [link](https://www.avesta.org/gathas.htm), and in [pdf](https://www.avesta.org/dastur/Dinshaw_J_Irani_The_Gathas.pdf) format.

**Which is the best translation of the Gathas?**

There is no single “best” translation of the Gathas, as each translator reflects different scholarly approaches, linguistic interpretations, and stylistic choices. Some prioritize literal fidelity to the Old Avestan language, while others aim for readability and poetic expression. Widely respected translations include Bartholomae (via Taraporewala) for linguistic accuracy, Darmesteter for literary elegance, and Martin Haug for historical significance. Many scholars recommend consulting more than one translation to capture the nuances of meaning, as well as the rhythm and theological depth of Zarathushtra’s hymns.

**Why do the Gathas start with Yasna 28?**

The Gathas are part of the Yasna, a larger liturgical collection in the Avesta. Within the Yasna, the Gathic hymns are numbered starting at **Yasna 28**, because the preceding chapters (Yasna 1–27) contain ritual texts, invocations, and ceremonial material that predate the hymns themselves. Conceptually, some scholars consider Yasna 29 as the “first” Gatha, since it introduces Zarathushtra’s revelation and mission. The numbering reflects the structure of the Yasna rather than the chronological or thematic order of the Gathas themselves.

**Are there any other known works of Zarathushtra in written form?**

No other works by Zarathushtra in his own hand are known to survive. The **Gathas**, preserved within the Yasna of the Avesta, are the only texts generally attributed directly to him. Other Avestan texts—ritual formulas, hymns, and commentaries—were composed later by followers and priests to explain, interpret, or expand on his teachings, but they are not considered his original compositions.
